Scene tagging uses the CLIP ViT-B/32 image encoder by OpenAI
(https://github.com/openai/CLIP), in the quantized ONNX export published at
https://huggingface.co/Xenova/clip-vit-base-patch32 (revision
d15189d7028b43f1d3e65039190477f6af591c2a). labels.json contains text
embeddings computed with the matching CLIP text encoder.
