Your idea for this video

Before anything is planned, tell us what you have in mind: the story, the jokes, which pictures or characters should appear and when, the tone. Your idea goes first; the program adds its own around it.

  1. Your talking video. The one where you talk to the camera (up to ten minutes works well).
  2. Close-ups of what you mention (optional): every other video or photo of the project. Film the things you talk about, close and steady, a few seconds each.
  3. Their voices (optional): while filming a thing, say its lines in its voice. In the review you choose which recorded line each character says.

Captions

Text, timing and caption style

Record, stop and review the text below. Separate phrases with blank lines, then distribute them: each phrase gets its own wild look (colour, size, outline, shadow, glow, place and effect), and long phrases, or phrases typed on two lines, are chained across overlapping captions.

Auto Read transcribes the mounted Reel itself, so its timestamps already match the final video. It creates editable captions and leaves the lyrics field alone.

SRT

Caption blocks

With the video playing, press Space to mark a caption: the first press starts it and the next closes it; new captions close automatically after 1 second.
