# After PR #44: wrong pictures, lost storyboard, full disk, watermarks — 21 October 2026

Stacked on `codex/show-texture-placement-audio` (PR #44). UI stays English; no private media in Git.

## What the person reported and what was found

1. **"I put a magnet and the render shows another sticker."** A library picture the director named
   (`params.kind == "photo"`) that the person replaced with their own picture was rendered from the
   library name, not from the chosen picture. A chosen picture now wins for every kind (`gags.make_items`).
2. **"The afro kid shows in the render but not when I play the scene, so I cannot move it."** Named
   library pictures were never linked to a file, so the live view had nothing to draw. They are linked
   (exact name only) when the storyboard opens (`_link_named_photos`), and the live view places every
   picture where the render draws it (`gags.placement`, sent as `params.shown`).
3. **"The movements (up, down, jump, grow, swing…) are gone."** They existed only for placed stickers,
   two menus deep. *Movement ▸* is now offered for drawings, words and library pictures too: in their
   right-click menu, the timeline bar's menu and the selected bar's toolbar (`params.action`).
4. **Watermarks** (asked in the Codex thread, left out there): see below.
5. **The final render failed with "No space left on device".** Clip inserts were decoded to raw frames
   in a scratch file before rendering (≈5 GB per 30 s of full HD). Clips are now read from FFmpeg while
   the render plays them (`show_inserts.ClipFrames`).
6. **The edited storyboard of "La prueba" was replaced.** The app log shows the project opened at
   scene 14 on 21 Oct 00:25, two failed final renders (disk full), then *Make it* (`/wizard/start`) at
   00:37, which planned the Show again and wrote 36 new proposals over the edited storyboard with no copy.
   Making the Show again now saves the creative documents first (`artifacts/history/<time>/`, newest 30),
   and *Earlier versions…* in the storyboard restores one (the current one is saved first).
   No copy of the lost state was found on disk; its rendered scene previews and chosen pictures remain.

## Watermarks

`core/media_framing.py` already samples nine frames of every imported clip to trim black edges. The same
samples now look for a watermark: a small mark in a corner whose outline is in (almost) every sample
while the clip changes around it, and whose own pixels change much less than its surroundings (≥1.8×);
scenery in a still shot changes like what is around it, so it is left alone. The clip is cut from the
mark's side, same shape, at most 1.3×. Clip settings say what was done and can keep the mark
(`<clip>.framing.json`; the choice also changes the effect so previews are not reused).
On the 63 clips of the test project it found the two real marks and nothing on the host's footage.
It is a heuristic: moving marks (bouncing TikTok logos) and marks over still shots are not handled.
